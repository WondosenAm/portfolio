About me

I started in 2017 as a trainee at Information Network Security Administration, building data warehouses and ETL in SQL Server and Oracle. Over six years I was promoted four times and finished as supervisor of a five-person visualization team.

In 2023 I moved into data science. At a bank I built a customer segmentation model from raw transaction data. As a freelancer I analyzed survey data for a marketing team in California, which used the results to change how it targeted and timed its campaigns.

Today I lead the data function at an international NGO, working with seven departments that cover commodity distribution, WASH, livelihoods and mental health programs. Most of my work is making reporting automatic and reliable, so every department’s monthly numbers agree.

I’ve also taught Python to high school students at a summer coding camp.
